# The River Remembers — Art Direction & Storyboard

Style bible: hand-painted luminous storybook, midnight indigo (#0b1038) to deep teal water, warm gold flame (#ffc46b), lotus pink (#f27fb0), moon-white, marigold. Soft painterly edges, glow, NO text, NO watermarks, NO real logos, Thai-cultural but gentle and modern. Portrait 9:16 first (mobile).

## 7 Acts (single continuous canvas, one scroll-free timeline; gestures advance, not a Next button)
1. Whispering River (0:00-0:30) — dark river; each touch makes a light ripple that wakes a star in the sky and a glow in the water. 8 touches (or 20 s of dwelling) awaken the whole river; fireflies follow the finger.
2. Create Krathong (0:30-1:05) — petals/flowers float up from the water; drag each onto the banana-leaf base; krathong assembles layer by layer, tiers glow when placed.
3. Living Flame (1:05-1:35) — hold to light the candle; flame breathes with press; the smoke/embers rise and become the recipient name, then the message, as living typography.
4. Memory Echo (1:35-2:10) — draw on the water surface; the stroke ripples and is remembered as a trail of light; after a few strokes the trails fly together into the recipient's name.
5. Impossible River (2:10-2:45) — tilt/drag: water and sky swap places; krathong sails across the sky, stars sink into the river (mirrored displacement of one field).
6. Release the Wish (2:45-3:15) — swipe to push the krathong off; type/choose a wish; it sails away leaving the wish as a glowing wake.
7. Grand Finale (3:15-3:45) — the river replays the player's own traces (ripples, strokes, krathong choices) as lanterns rising and a personalised sky: name constellation, message, count of touches.
